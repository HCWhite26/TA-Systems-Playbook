# Screening SOP

---

## 🎯 Purpose  
Ensure every recruiter screen is structured, evidence‑based, and aligned with the success profile, must‑haves, and dealbreakers defined during Intake and HM Alignment.  
This SOP defines how TA evaluates candidates consistently and objectively before advancing them to the Hiring Manager.

---

## 📌 Principles  
- **Evidence over impressions** — no vibes, no narratives, no assumptions  
- **Consistency over improvisation** — same structure, same signals, every time  
- **Clarity over ambiguity** — candidates should know what to expect  
- **Efficiency over volume** — screens are purposeful, not exploratory  
- **Alignment over autonomy** — screens reflect HM expectations, not recruiter preference  

---

## 🧩 When Screening Is Required  

A recruiter screen is required for:

- Inbound applicants who meet minimum qualifications  
- Sourced candidates before HM review  
- Referrals (unless HM explicitly waives)  
- Candidates whose resume suggests partial alignment but needs clarification  

A screen may be **skipped** only when:

- The HM has already spoken to the candidate  
- The candidate is internal and already vetted  
- The HM explicitly requests a direct interview (rare and documented)  

---

## 📝 Inputs Required Before Screening  

TA must review:

- Intake summary  
- Success profile (30/60/90 + 1‑year outcomes)  
- Must‑haves vs. nice‑to‑haves  
- Dealbreakers  
- Compensation band  
- HM Alignment notes  
- Any updated role nuances  

---

# 🗂️ Screening Structure (30 Minutes)

---

## 1. Introduction (2 minutes)  

**Purpose:** Set context and build quick rapport.  

TA covers:

- Who they are and their role  
- Brief overview of the company/team  
- Brief overview of the role  
- What this conversation will cover  

---

## 2. Career Narrative (5–7 minutes)  

**Purpose:** Understand the candidate’s trajectory and context.  

TA asks:

- “Walk me through your background at a high level.”  
- “What led you to your current role?”  
- “What prompted you to explore new opportunities now?”  

Signals:

- Clarity of narrative  
- Ownership of decisions  
- Relevance to role scope  

---

## 3. Role Alignment (8–10 minutes)  

**Purpose:** Assess alignment with core requirements and success profile.  

TA probes:

- Relevant experience (scope, scale, complexity)  
- Tools, systems, or domains required for the role  
- Examples of work that map to success outcomes  

Signals:

- Direct alignment with must‑haves  
- Evidence of similar scope/complexity  
- Ability to articulate impact  

---

## 4. Motivation & Constraints (5–7 minutes)  

**Purpose:** Understand why this role, why now, and what might block a hire.  

TA asks:

- “What about this role is most interesting to you?”  
- “What would you need to see to feel this is the right move?”  
- “What are your non‑negotiables?”  

TA clarifies:

- Location / remote expectations  
- Work authorization  
- Travel expectations (if applicable)  

Signals:

- Genuine interest vs. generic exploration  
- Alignment with role realities  
- Potential dealbreakers  

---

## 5. Compensation & Leveling (3–5 minutes)  

**Purpose:** Confirm alignment with band and level early.  

TA covers:

- Current compensation (if appropriate and compliant)  
- Desired compensation range  
- Openness

# Sourcing SOP

---

## 🎯 Purpose  
Build a proactive, targeted, and data‑driven sourcing strategy that delivers qualified, aligned candidates into the pipeline.  
This SOP defines how TA identifies, engages, and nurtures talent using structured methods, market intelligence, and repeatable workflows.

---

## 📌 Principles  
- **Proactive over reactive** — sourcing begins before the pipeline dries up  
- **Targeted over broad** — quality > volume  
- **Data over intuition** — decisions guided by patterns, not guesses  
- **Consistency over chaos** — repeatable workflows outperform ad‑hoc searching  
- **Engagement over outreach** — personalized messaging wins  

---

## 🧩 When Sourcing Is Required  
Sourcing is required when:

- The role is hard‑to‑fill  
- Inbound applicants are low‑quality or low‑volume  
- The HM needs a diverse slate  
- The role requires niche skills or experience  
- The search is time‑sensitive  
- Market validation indicates limited active talent  

Sourcing is optional only when:

- Inbound pipeline is consistently strong  
- HM has a referral pipeline  
- The role is high‑volume and easily filled  

---

## 📝 Inputs Required Before Sourcing  
TA must review:

- Intake Summary  
- Success Profile (30/60/90 + 1‑year outcomes)  
- Must‑haves vs. nice‑to‑haves  
- Dealbreakers  
- Compensation band  
- Market validation insights  
- HM Alignment notes  
- Competitor list  

---

# 🗂️ Sourcing Workflow

---

## 1. Build the Target Profile  
Define:

- Required skills  
- Required experience  
- Required tools/systems  
- Industry alignment  
- Level/scope  
- Geographic constraints  
- Compensation alignment  

Output: **Searchable criteria** for Boolean, filters, and platforms.

---

## 2. Identify Target Companies  
Use:

- Competitors  
- Adjacent industries  
- Companies with similar org structures  
- Companies with similar tech stacks  
- Companies known for producing strong talent  

Categories:

- **Primary targets** — direct competitors  
- **Secondary targets** — adjacent industries  
- **Tertiary targets** — wildcard sources  

---

# Interview Loop SOP

---

## 🎯 Purpose  
Establish a consistent, calibrated, and signal‑driven interview process that produces high‑quality, unbiased hiring decisions.  
This SOP defines how interview loops are designed, executed, and evaluated.

---

## 📌 Principles  
- **Consistency over preference** — interviewers follow the loop, not personal style  
- **Signals over stories** — decisions are based on evidence, not vibes  
- **Calibration over speed** — a fast loop is useless if misaligned  
- **Structure over improvisation** — every stage has a purpose and a defined signal  

---

## 🧩 When to Build or Update an Interview Loop  
A loop must be created or updated when:

- A new role is opened  
- A role is leveled up or down  
- The scope of the role changes  
- Interviewers are new or uncalibrated  
- HM Alignment identifies drift  
- A search stalls due to inconsistent feedback  

---

## 📝 Inputs Required Before Building the Loop  
TA gathers:

- Success profile (30/60/90 + 1‑year outcomes)  
- Must‑haves vs. nice‑to‑haves  
- Decision criteria  
- Technical + behavioral competencies  
- HM Alignment notes  
- Market validation insights  

---

# 🗂️ Interview Loop Structure

---

## 1. Recruiter Screen (TA)  
**Purpose:** Validate fundamentals before HM time is used.  
**Signals Assessed:**  
- Role alignment  
- Experience relevance  
- Communication clarity  
- Compensation alignment  
- Motivation & constraints  
- Dealbreakers  

---

## 2. Hiring Manager Screen  
**Purpose:** Validate technical depth + role fit.  
**Signals Assessed:**  
- Core competencies  
- Problem‑solving  
- Ownership  
- Technical depth  
- Expectations alignment  

---

## 3. Technical / Functional Interview(s)  
**Purpose:** Assess the candidate’s ability to perform the work.  
**Signals Assessed:**  
- Technical execution  
- Systems thinking  
- Analytical reasoning  
- Tool proficiency  
- Scenario‑based problem solving  

---

## 4. Cross‑Functional Interview  
**Purpose:** Validate collaboration, communication, and cross‑team impact.  
**Signals Assessed:**  
- Stakeholder management  
- Communication clarity  
- Conflict navigation  
- Influence without authority  

---

## 5. Behavioral / Values Interview  
**Purpose:** Assess alignment with team norms and organizational values.  
**Signals Assessed:**  
- Decision‑making  
- Ownership  
- Adaptability  
- Integrity  
- Learning mindset  

---

## 6. Final Decision Review  
**Purpose:** Synthesize all signals into a single hiring decision.  
**Inputs:**  
- Interviewer scorecards  
- Written evidence  
- Calibration notes  
- HM recommendation  
- TA recommendation  
- Risks & constraints  

---

# 👥 Interviewer Responsibilities  
Interviewers must:

- Review the job description + success profile  
- Review assigned signals before the interview  
- Ask structured, repeatable questions  
- Take notes in real time  
- Submit scorecards within 24 hours  
- Provide evidence‑based feedback  
- Avoid duplicating signals covered by others  

---

# 🎛️ TA Responsibilities  
TA must:

- Build the loop  
- Assign interviewers  
- Provide interviewer calibration  
- Ensure interviewers understand their signals  
- Monitor feedback quality  
- Identify drift or misalignment  
- Facilitate the final decision review  

---

## 🔧 Calibration Requirements  
Calibration is required when:

- A new interviewer joins  
- A role is new or evolving  
- Interviewers disagree on signals  
- Feedback quality is inconsistent  
- A search stalls due to unclear bar  

Calibration includes:

- Reviewing sample answers  
- Reviewing what “good” vs. “bad” looks like  
- Reviewing scoring guidelines  
- Reviewing past candidate patterns  

---

## 🧭 Decision Criteria  
A candidate must meet:

- All **must‑have** competencies  
- The **success profile** expectations  
- **Compensation alignment**  
- **Role scope** expectations  

A candidate may still be rejected if:

- Signals conflict  
- Evidence is weak  
- Interviewers disagree on fundamentals  
- Risks outweigh strengths  

---

## 🗣️ Standard Interview Loop Statement  
Add this to the search record:

> **This interview loop is the source of truth for all interview stages, signals, and decision criteria. Any changes must be aligned with TA and HM before implementation.**

---

## 🚨 Risks & Escalation  
Escalate when:

- Interviewers are not submitting scorecards  
- Interviewers ask unstructured or off‑loop questions  
- HM attempts to change the loop mid‑search  
- Feedback is inconsistent or biased  
- The loop produces false positives or false negatives

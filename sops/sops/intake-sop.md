# Intake SOP

---

## 🎯 Purpose  
Establish a structured, repeatable, and strategic intake process that ensures Talent Acquisition (TA) and the Hiring Manager (HM) are aligned on role expectations, success outcomes, must‑haves, dealbreakers, compensation, and market realities before the search begins.

The intake meeting becomes the **source of truth** for the entire search.

---

## 📌 Principles  
- **Clarity over assumptions** — nothing is left vague or implied  
- **Alignment over speed** — a fast start is useless if misaligned  
- **Outcomes over tasks** — define success, not just responsibilities  
- **Market reality over wishlists** — TA guides feasibility  
- **Documentation over memory** — everything is captured in the Intake Summary  

---

## 🧩 When Intake Is Required  
An intake meeting is required when:

- A new role is opened  
- A backfill is requested  
- A role is re‑leveled  
- A role is re‑scoped  
- A search is being restarted  
- A HM is new or unfamiliar with TA processes  

Intake may be skipped only when:

- The role is identical to a recently filled position  
- HM and TA agree no changes are needed  
- Documentation from the previous intake is still valid  

---

## 📝 Inputs Required Before Intake  
TA must review:

- Previous job description  
- Previous intake notes (if applicable)  
- Org structure  
- Compensation band  
- Market data  
- HM’s initial request  
- Any internal equity considerations  

---

# 🗂️ Intake Meeting Structure (45–60 Minutes)

---

## 1. Role Context & Business Need  
**Purpose:** Understand *why* the role exists.

TA asks:

- “What business problem does this role solve?”  
- “Is this growth, backfill, or restructuring?”  
- “What happens if we don’t fill this role?”  

Outputs:

- Business justification  
- Urgency level  
- Dependencies  

---

## 2. Success Profile (30/60/90 + 1‑Year Outcomes)  
**Purpose:** Define measurable success, not vague expectations.

TA asks:

- “What does success look like in the first 30, 60, 90 days?”  
- “What must this person accomplish in their first year?”  
- “How will you measure success?”  

Outputs:

- Success profile  
- Performance indicators  
- Clear expectations  

---

## 3. Must‑Haves vs. Nice‑to‑Haves  
**Purpose:** Prevent HM wishlists from derailing the search.

TA asks:

- “Which skills are absolutely required?”  
- “Which skills are preferred but not mandatory?”  
- “What are dealbreakers?”  

Outputs:

- Must‑have competencies  
- Nice‑to‑have competencies  
- Dealbreakers  

---

## 4. Role Scope & Leveling  
**Purpose:** Ensure the role level matches expectations.

TA asks:

- “Is this an IC or leadership role?”  
- “What level of autonomy is expected?”  
- “What decisions will this person own?”  

Outputs:

- Confirmed level  
- Scope clarity  
- Decision‑making authority  

---

## 5. Compensation & Budget  
**Purpose:** Align expectations with approved bands.

TA covers:

- Approved compensation band  
- Bonus/commission structure  
- Equity (if applicable)  
- Sign‑on considerations  
- Internal equity constraints  

TA asks:

- “Is the band flexible?”  
- “What is the walk‑away point?”  

Outputs:

- Compensation alignment  
- Flexibility notes  
- Risks  

---

## 6. Interview Plan & Signals  
**Purpose:** Define the structure before sourcing begins.

TA asks:

- “Who needs to be on the loop?”  
- “What signals should each interviewer assess?”  
- “What competencies matter most?”  

Outputs:

- Interview loop draft  
- Assigned signals  
- Calibration needs  

---

## 7. Market Realities & Feasibility  
**Purpose:** Align HM expectations with actual talent availability.

TA covers:

- Market data  
- Talent pool size  
- Competitor demand  
- Compensation competitiveness  
- Geographic constraints  

Outputs:

- Feasibility assessment  
- Risks & constraints  
- Adjustments to expectations  

---

## 8. Sourcing Strategy  
**Purpose:** Define where and how TA will find talent.

TA covers:

- Target companies  
- Boolean strategy  
- Channels  
- Diversity considerations  
- Timeline expectations  

Outputs:

- Sourcing plan  
- Target profile  
- Outreach strategy  

---

## 9. Process Expectations  
**Purpose:** Prevent delays and miscommunication.

TA clarifies:

- Feedback SLAs  
- Scorecard expectations  
- HM availability  
- Communication cadence  
- Alignment checkpoints  

Outputs:

- Agreed SLAs  
- Communication plan  
- Alignment cadence  

---

# 🧾 Intake Summary (Required Output)

TA documents:

- Business need  
- Success profile  
- Must‑haves / nice‑to‑haves  
- Dealbreakers  
- Level & scope  
- Compensation alignment  
- Interview plan  
- Sourcing strategy  
- Risks & constraints  
- SLAs & expectations  

This becomes the **source of truth** for the search.

---

## 🗣️ Standard Intake Statement  

Add this to the search record:

> **“Intake completed. Success profile, must‑haves, compensation, interview plan, and sourcing strategy aligned with HM. This document is the source of truth for the search.”**

---

## 🚨 Risks & Escalation  
Escalate when:

- HM expectations conflict with market reality  
- Compensation is below market  
- Role scope is unclear or shifting  
- HM is unresponsive  
- Success profile is not measurable  
- Search stalls due to misalignment  

---

## 📊 KPIs  
- Time from intake → sourcing start  
- Intake completeness score  
- HM satisfaction with intake process  
- Search success rate tied to intake quality  
- Number of escalations due to misalignment

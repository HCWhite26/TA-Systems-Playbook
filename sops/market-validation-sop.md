# Market Validation SOP — v1  
A workforce-planning control point that validates role viability before intake and before opening a requisition.

---

## 1. Purpose
Market validation ensures that every role entering the TA workflow is feasible, aligned with market realities, and supported by data.  
It exists to prevent misaligned, unfunded, or unrealistic requisitions from entering the recruiting pipeline.

Market validation is a **workforce planning safeguard**, not a sourcing task.

---

## 2. How Market Validation Connects to Workforce Planning

Workforce planning should answer:
- What roles are needed?
- Why are they needed?
- When are they needed?
- What level and scope?
- What budget exists?

In practice, these steps are often incomplete or skipped.  
Market validation fills the gap by confirming:

1. **The role is actually needed**  
   - Business justification  
   - Alignment to headcount plan  
   - Funding confirmed  

2. **The role is defined correctly**  
   - Title calibration  
   - Level alignment  
   - Scope vs. compensation  

3. **The market can support the hire**  
   - Talent pool availability  
   - Geographic feasibility  
   - Competitive landscape  

4. **The timeline is realistic**  
   - Fill difficulty  
   - Market scarcity  
   - Internal constraints  

5. **The req should move forward**  
   - Go / No-Go recommendation  
   - Required adjustments before intake  

---

## 3. Workflow Position  
Market validation occurs **before intake** and **before a req is opened**.

Workflow sequence:
1. Workforce Planning  
2. Market Validation  
3. Intake Meeting  
4. Requisition Creation  
5. Sourcing Strategy  
6. Recruiting Execution  

---

## 4. When to Use This SOP
Run market validation before:
- Opening a new requisition  
- Accepting an intake meeting  
- Hiring for a new role, new location, or new skillset  
- Roles with unclear compensation or level  
- Situations where hiring manager expectations require grounding  

---

## 5. Required Inputs
Collect the following before starting:
- Job title and level  
- Location (onsite, hybrid, remote)  
- Compensation range  
- Must-have skills  
- Nice-to-have skills  
- Hiring timeline  
- Internal constraints (budget, clearance, shift, travel, etc.)  

---

## 6. Validation Steps

### Step 1 — Title and Level Calibration
- Compare proposed title to market-standard equivalents  
- Identify competitor titles for similar scope  
- Flag mismatches between level expectations and compensation  

### Step 2 — Talent Pool Availability
- Estimate talent pool size  
- Assess active vs. passive talent ratio  
- Identify geographic density  
- Review seniority distribution  

### Step 3 — Compensation Benchmarking
- Compare range to market median  
- Identify gaps that impact fillability  
- Recommend adjustments when needed  

### Step 4 — Competitive Landscape
- Identify top competitors hiring for similar roles  
- Review hiring surges or freezes  
- Note competitor value propositions  
- Identify differentiators for your organization  

### Step 5 — Risk Assessment
Evaluate:  
- Market scarcity  
- Compensation competitiveness  
- Timeline feasibility  
- Internal constraints  
- Overall fill difficulty  

Output: **Red / Yellow / Green** rating.

---

## 7. Deliverables
Produce a Market Validation Summary including:
- Title calibration findings  
- Talent pool snapshot  
- Compensation alignment  
- Competitor analysis  
- Risk rating  
- Recommended adjustments  
- Go / No-Go recommendation for intake  

---

## 8. Intake Meeting Readiness
Proceed to intake only when:
- Data is validated  
- Risks are documented  
- Hiring manager expectations are aligned  
- Compensation is confirmed  
- Role is feasible within timeline

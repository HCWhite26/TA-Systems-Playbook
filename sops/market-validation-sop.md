# Market Validation SOP — v1
A standardized process for validating role viability before intake and before opening a requisition.

## 1. Purpose
Ensure each role is feasible, aligned with market realities, and supported by data before entering the TA workflow. Market validation reduces wasted cycles, prevents misaligned reqs, and equips TA with evidence to influence hiring decisions.

## 2. When to Use This SOP
Conduct market validation before:
- Opening a new requisition
- Accepting an intake meeting
- Hiring for a new role, new location, or new skillset
- Roles with unclear compensation or level
- Situations where hiring manager expectations require grounding

## 3. Required Inputs
Collect the following before starting:
- Job title and level
- Location (onsite, hybrid, remote)
- Compensation range
- Must-have skills
- Nice-to-have skills
- Hiring timeline
- Internal constraints (budget, clearance, shift, travel, etc.)

## 4. Validation Steps

### Step 1 — Title and Level Calibration
Purpose: Ensure the role aligns with market naming conventions.
- Compare proposed title to market-standard equivalents
- Identify competitor titles for similar scope
- Flag mismatches between level expectations and compensation

### Step 2 — Talent Pool Availability
Purpose: Determine whether the market can support the role.
- Estimate talent pool size
- Assess active vs. passive talent ratio
- Identify geographic density
- Review seniority distribution

### Step 3 — Compensation Benchmarking
Purpose: Validate competitiveness of the proposed range.
- Compare range to market median
- Identify gaps that impact fillability
- Recommend adjustments when needed

### Step 4 — Competitive Landscape
Purpose: Understand who else is hiring and at what velocity.
- Identify top competitors hiring for similar roles
- Review hiring surges or freezes
- Note competitor value propositions
- Identify differentiators for your organization
